This policy explains what personal information Axencraft collects, why, who we share it with and your rights. We keep it as short and plain as we can.

1. Who we are

Axencraft is the trading name of Freddie Joseph Green, a sole trader based in England. Our address is Upover, Stockleigh English, Crediton, EX17 4DD, United Kingdom. You can email us at support@axencraft.co.uk. We're not VAT registered, so our prices don't include VAT.

We are the "controller" of the personal information described here. For messages sent through our customers' websites, see section 7.

3. Emails we send

We email you about your account and website, for example when your preview is ready, when your website goes live, when a change is done, before a free trial ends and if a payment fails. These are part of the service, so you can't opt out of them while you have a plan. We'll only send marketing emails if you've agreed to them.

4. Who we share it with

We use trusted providers to run our service. They only use your information to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: our database, logins and file storage
  • Vercel: hosting for our website and our customers' websites
  • Stripe: payments and billing
  • Resend: sending emails
  • ImprovMX and Google (Gmail): receiving and managing email sent to support@axencraft.co.uk
  • Anthropic: if we use AI to help draft website wording, your questionnaire answers may be sent to Anthropic's Claude for this purpose
  • Google Fonts: our pages load fonts from Google, which means your IP address is shared with Google when a page loads

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights.

5. International transfers

Some of these providers are based in, or use servers in, the United States or elsewhere outside the UK. Where this happens, we rely on legal safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK government's adequacy regulations (including the UK-US data bridge) or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account and website: while your account is active, and for up to 12 months after your plan ends so you can come back. After that we may delete your website, questionnaire answers and uploads.
  • Payment and billing records: 6 years, because UK tax law requires it.
  • Waitlist: until the studio opens and we've told you, or until you ask us to remove you.
  • Server logs: kept for a short time by our providers for security, usually no longer than 30 days.

7. Our customers' websites

When someone fills in a contact, booking or newsletter form on a website we host for one of our customers, we pass their details to that business by email. We don't keep a copy of the message. For this information the business is the controller and we act on its behalf. If you sent a message through one of those websites, please contact that business about how it uses your details. To prevent spam, we keep a scrambled version of the sender's IP address for up to 30 days.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to or ask us to restrict how we use it;
  • ask for your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it (such as the waitlist).

To use any of these rights, email support@axencraft.co.uk. We'll reply within one month. Some rights have limits; for example, we must keep billing records for tax purposes.

9. Keeping it safe

We use secure, encrypted connections, access controls so that customers can only see their own information, and reputable providers with strong security. Uploaded files are stored privately until they're published on your website.

10. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when our service changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and we'll email you about important changes.
