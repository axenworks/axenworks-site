How Digital Studio works

You choose and answer. We build, check and host.

  1. Pick a plan and styleChoose your plan, pay a one-off setup fee, then pick from 7 styles.
  2. Answer the questionnaireTell us about your business, style, pages and content.
  3. We build your draftYour answers and template become a custom website.
  4. We review itEvery draft is checked and edited by hand before you see it.
  5. Preview and request changesView a private preview and ask for what you'd like changed.
  6. Go liveOnce you approve, we host your site as your own website.
