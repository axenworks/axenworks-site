Questions

Can't see yours? Email support@axencraft.co.uk.

What do I pay today?

Just the one-off £50 setup fee. Your monthly plan doesn't start until your website is live.

What do I need to get started?

An email address and a few details about your business. After payment, a short questionnaire asks about your business, your style and your words. You can add your logo and photos, and your answers save as you go so you can finish later.

Can I see my website before it goes live?

Yes. You'll get a private preview link in your account. Ask for as many changes as you like while you're reviewing it. Nothing goes live until you're happy.

How do changes work after my site is live?

Request a change from your account. Each plan includes some changes every month (1, 3 or 6), and extra ones are £10 each. We'll always ask before charging for an extra change.

Where will my website live?

We host it for you at an address like yourbusiness.axencraft.co.uk. If you have your own domain name, you can connect it from your account.

Can I change plan or cancel?

Yes. Use "Manage billing" in your account to switch plan, update your card, see invoices or cancel.

Hear when Print and Grow open

Print Studio and Grow Studio are still on the bench. Leave your email and we'll tell you when they open. That's the only thing we'll use it for.
