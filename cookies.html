This page explains the small amount of information our website stores in your browser. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, so we don't need to ask for your permission with a cookie banner.

1. What we store and why

Websites can store small pieces of information in your browser using cookies or "local storage". We only store what's needed for the website to work:

NameTypeWhat it's forHow long
sb-…-auth-tokenLocal storageKeeps you logged in to your account (Supabase)Until you log out
axc-plan, axc-styleLocal storageRemembers the plan and style you chose on our homepage, ready for checkout and the questionnaireUntil you pay, or clear your browser
axc-goSession storageTakes you straight to payment after you create your accountUntil you close the tab
axw-q-stepLocal storageRemembers which step of the questionnaire you were onUntil you send the questionnaire
axw-theme:…Local storageRemembers light or dark mode on Pro websitesUntil you clear your browser

These are "strictly necessary" for the features you use, so the law doesn't require consent for them.

2. Other companies

  • Stripe sets its own cookies on its secure checkout and billing pages to process payments and prevent fraud. See Stripe's cookie information.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts on our pages. Google receives your IP address when fonts load, but doesn't set cookies for this.

3. Controlling storage

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. If you do, you'll be logged out and may need to choose your plan or style again.

4. Changes

If we ever add analytics or anything that isn't strictly necessary, we'll update this page and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.
