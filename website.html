Websites

What do I pay today?

Just the one-off £50 setup fee. Your plan doesn't start until your website is live.

What do I need to get started?

An email address and a few details about your business. After payment, a short questionnaire asks about your business, your style and your words. You can add your logo and photos, and your answers save as you go so you can finish later.

Can I see my website before it goes live?

Yes. You'll get a private preview link in your account. Ask for as many changes as you like while you're reviewing it. Nothing goes live until you're happy.

How long does it take to build?

Your first draft is ready to preview within 7 days of you finishing the questionnaire. We check every draft by hand before you see it.

How do changes work after my site is live?

There are two ways. AI edits: in your account, type what you'd like changed, such as your opening hours, a price or a colour. You see a before and after, then publish it or undo it. They're included in every plan up to a fair-use limit, and you can buy more if you run out. Change requests: for bigger jobs like new photos or pages, ask us and we'll do it. Each plan includes some every month (1, 3 or 6), and extra ones are £10 each. We'll always ask before charging for an extra change. Changes while you're reviewing your preview are free and don't use either.

Where will my website live, and is a domain name included?

We host it for you, over a secure (HTTPS) connection, at an address like yourbusiness.axencraft.co.uk, and that's included in your plan. If you'd rather use your own domain name, you can connect it from your account. You'd register and renew that with your domain provider and pay them directly.

Who owns my website?

You own your words, logos and photos, and you can download them from your account at any time. The templates, designs and code belong to us, so they can't be copied or used elsewhere, and you can use them as part of your website while your plan is active. The full detail is in our terms.

Can I change plan or cancel?

Yes. Use "Manage billing" in your account to switch plan, update your card, see invoices or cancel. Your website stays online until the end of the month you've paid for. We keep it saved for up to 12 months, so you can come back, and you can download your words and photos before then.

Can I get the setup fee back?

Because we start building as soon as you've paid, the £50 setup fee isn't refundable once we've started, unless we can't deliver the service. If you come back after cancelling while your website is still saved, you won't pay it again.
